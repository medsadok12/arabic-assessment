# Arem Academy project instructions

- User-facing training numbers must use Western digits `0–9`, including in Arabic interfaces. This is Mohamed's standing requirement (2026-10-31) for current training edits and all future training work.
- Use `lms/lib/display-number.js` for counters, scores, progress, rewards and result totals. With `Intl` formatters explicitly set `numberingSystem: 'latn'`.
- Do not convert display numbers to Arabic-Indic or Persian digits. Keep Arabic text and RTL layout intact.
- Changing number presentation must not modify stored points, calculations, answer data, media, animations or unrelated exercises.
- Read the relevant project context in `CLAUDE.md` before broader project changes. Preserve the user's requested scope.

- Standing requirement from Mohamed (2026-11-02): every optional training feature, uploaded media item, recording, interaction sound and visual effect must have independent enable/disable controls in the admin/teacher editor. Disabling must preserve uploaded file URLs and configured values, suppress built-in fallback sounds too, persist through draft/publish and match live preview. Keep core navigation usable; do not remove data to simulate disabling. Apply this to every new training and future edits to existing trainings.

- Standing visual continuity requirement from Mohamed (2026-11-05): keep each training world a single coherent full-screen scene across stage selection, gate opening, character arrival, celebration and return. Preserve the active portrait/landscape canvas, camera scale, landmark placement and aspect ratio through transitions. Never replace a landscape scene with a narrow portrait panel over a second enlarged background, repeat scenery at side seams, or introduce detached margins during celebrations. Use matching responsive assets and in-scene overlays; verify desktop, tablet and phone, including the celebration itself, before publishing.

- Character emergence QA (Mohamed, 2026-11-05): gate occlusion masks must cover only structural arch/columns/leaves, never a strip of the path or stepping stones. Check the first walking frames on phone and desktop; terrain must not suddenly paint over the character during emergence.

- Agreed academic progression (Mohamed, 2026-11-06): stages 1/2/3 show exactly 2/3/4 total answer choices, including the correct answer, across all vocabulary worlds. Word targets stay 5/10/15. The editor, stage summaries, additions, stored configuration, staff previews and student questions must agree. A visual redesign must not change this methodology. The home suggestion toggle selects distractor content; it must not change the stage's number of choices.

- Letter Catcher reading convention (Mohamed, 2026-11-17): in student-facing word tiles and completed words, omit the short vowel before a matching plain long-vowel letter (e.g. زَرافَةٌ، فيلٌ، عُصْفورٌ). Keep other vowels, sukun, shadda and endings; do not treat consonantal و/ي as madd. Preserve the fully vocalized source for pronunciation, teacher editing and answer checks.

- Letter Catcher animal-only trial (Mohamed, 2026-11-22): display animal words in one shared frame using native Naskh text and the original contextual letter joins around the missing letter. Use the single `MissingLetterMark` component at its fixed 16×26 CSS-pixel size for every word: gold hook, its own red dot, no underline or extra dot. Preserve each letter's real diacritics and remove the hidden letter's marks with it. Keep all other categories on their existing layout until Mohamed explicitly requests expansion; apply the gate to the actual word category, including mixed rounds. Check marker dimensions across words and phone/tablet/desktop before shipping.
- Animal-word dammatan (Mohamed, 2026-11-22): render the current Noto Naskh Arabic font's native open dammatan U+08F1 for two visibly separate dammas, in both missing-letter frames and revealed/completed animal words. Apply `withSeparatedDammatan` only to display strings before measuring/rendering; preserve U+064C in stored words, speech, answer checks and accessible labels. Keep the font, other marks, letter joins and other categories unchanged.
- Fahim overlay in every Letter Catcher category (Mohamed's explicit expansion, 2026-11-24; supersedes the animal-only mascot trial of 2026-11-22): show the moving mascot as a transparent overlay at the upper right of the encouragement row, then dismiss it after his speech. Apply this appearance to every canonical and custom word category, including resumed mixed rounds. Never translate or scale the vocabulary picture or reflow the word/answer controls. Motion must still work when audio is blocked, and leaving/restarting must cancel old playback. This expansion changes the mascot only: the animal-only word frame, missing-letter marker and separated dammatan remain restricted to animals, and friend duels remain animal-only.

- Letter Catcher friend duel (Mohamed, 2026-11-22): keep «تحدي صديق» limited to two players and real animal words until expansion is explicitly requested. Default to 10 questions, with teacher selection from 5 through 10 and exactly 3 answer choices. Preserve the approved animal word frame, Naskh font, contextual joins, separated dammatan and fixed missing-letter marker. Keep independent teacher controls for availability, title, description, sound, motion and encouragement; disabling preserves configured values and media. Preserve single-player training and every other category.
- Letter Catcher duel points (Mohamed, 2026-11-22): per question, award 15 for the fastest correct first attempt, 10 for a later correct first attempt, and 5 for a correct answer after retrying. First-attempt answers within 500ms of the fastest share 15 points; enforce a 700ms retry cooldown. Wrong or unanswered questions earn 0, with no point deductions. The server owns clocks, answers, CAS updates and idempotent final credit; staff-preview rooms never award account points.
- Letter Catcher duel lifecycle: use server-generated 6-digit room codes and trusted active-child identity. Keep room snapshots server-only behind restrictive RLS and membership-checked APIs. Teacher reset softly cancels open rooms, preserving completed results, credited points, words, recordings and settings. See `lms/docs/letter-catcher-duel.md` for the current contract and release checks.
- Letter Catcher account photos (Mohamed, 2026-11-23): replace fixed child illustrations in the friend invitation with the active account's uploaded profile photo and an unknown-friend placeholder. Once joined, both screens show each participant's own profile photo beside their name and points, including waiting and results. Resolve photos from the verified active identity; never inherit a guardian/sibling photo or accept participant photos from a request body. Use a neutral per-account fallback for missing or failed photos, preserve circular cropping and the existing game design.

- Letter Catcher world-path selection (Mohamed, 2026-11-23; play-all removed by the 2026-11-24 clarification below): use the approved third concept, «مسار العوالم», as an island path for the start-screen world selector only. Build the path from live canonical categories in their existing order, including custom categories, live teacher images/emojis, saved student results, stars and existing unlock rules. Preserve teacher access bypass, selection callbacks and the existing optional friend-duel entry/settings. Do not hardcode a subset of worlds or change questions, answers, scoring, stored data or the in-game experience to fit the design. See `lms/docs/letter-catcher-world-path.md`.

- Letter Catcher topic-only progression (Mohamed's explicit clarification, 2026-11-24): selecting «الحيوانات» starts playing all playable animal words directly, and the same applies to every topic. Progression is between topics only; do not insert another stage selector, word-count chunks or internal locks within a topic. Keep «العب الكل» removed. The earlier default split into 5-word internal stages was the assistant's implementation choice, not an agreed teaching method, and Mohamed explicitly rejected it. Retain old settings and saved-round support where needed for existing data, but they must not divide new topic play. Preserve the live word bank, media, points, child-scoped progress, historical access and friend-duel behavior.

- Letter Catcher first-load artwork (Mohamed, 2026-11-24): every world must display its configured final artwork without first flashing a stock lion, default emoji or other substitute illustration. While live category metadata or a configured image is loading, show a neutral reserved loading state; an image failure must not switch to a different representational picture. Metadata failures are explicit retryable failures, not successful empty configuration. Preserve good displayed artwork during background refresh, reject stale responses, and keep the world layout stable. A category genuinely configured without an image may use its final configured/default emoji only after metadata has loaded successfully.
